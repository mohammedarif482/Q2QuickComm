What is QComm?

QComm is a quick-commerce platform for hypermarkets and retail chains. It adds a 30-minute delivery layer on top of your existing POS and stores, with live inventory sync, AI-powered search, and a branded customer app — enabling you to compete with Zepto and Blinkit using your own infrastructure.

Key Features

  • Live catalog synced from your POS/billing system
  • One branded app routing orders to nearest store
  • Operations-ready store app with pick lists and rider handover
  • AI-powered smart search, auto-enriched catalog, and recommendations
  • Promotional space for digital shelf advertising
